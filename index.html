BEFORE YOU PLAY

A few useful
things to know.

Can beginners join?

Yes. The club welcomes players of all ages and abilities. If you’re unsure which session suits you, contact the club before booking.

How do I book at Mulgrave Road?

New players should register for a Club ID and allow 24 hours for processing. Then use the online booking page. The first 48 bookings pay $10; the last six pay $15.

How do I book at Smithfield?

Text Maggie on 0469 931 390. Wednesday play runs from 6–7:30 pm. Payment is by EFT only, and players sign in on arrival.

How does the playing-credit bonus work?

For Mulgrave Road, pay $50 for $60 credit or $100 for $140 credit. Use your Club ID as your payment reference. See the club’s payment information for bank and PayID details.
